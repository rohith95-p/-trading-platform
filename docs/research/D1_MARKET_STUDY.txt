VERDICT  (written 2026-09-06, against D1_SYSTEM_PLAN.md's own Phase 1 gate)
========================================================================

GATE (from the plan): "at least ONE relationship is statistically real
(t-stat > 2, stable across decades) AND the random-entry control leaves room
for an edge."

RESULT: **CONDITIONAL GO -- on H2 (DXY) only. H1, H3 and H4 are rejected as
specified.** Three of the plan's four pre-registered hypotheses die here, and
they die on the plan's own criteria, before a line of strategy code was
written. That is the process working exactly as intended.

--- autocorrelation: MOMENTUM FAMILY DEAD (same as M15) ---
Only lag-40 (-0.0251) clears the +/-0.0248 significance line, marginally, and
it is NEGATIVE (reversion). Across 8 lags tested, one marginal hit at 2 s.e.
is what chance alone produces. Nothing here supports a D1 momentum family.

--- H1 TIME-SERIES MOMENTUM: REJECTED (sign is backwards) ---
The plan's headline, most-evidence-backed hypothesis. Measured: significant
REVERSION at 21d (beta -0.0939, t -7.56), 63d (t -3.72) and 126d (t -2.88).
The only momentum-signed lookback (252d) is not significant (t +1.36) and its
sign-agreement is unstable by period -- 62.8% (2000-07) -> 47.4% (2018-21),
i.e. worse than a coin flip in a full 4-year stretch. H1 as written cannot be
traded.
  ** Worth noting, NOT as a green light: the 21d reversion (t -7.56) is the
  single strongest statistical signal in this entire study. It is also
  entirely unregistered -- it was not one of the four hypotheses. Trading it
  now, having found it by looking, is precisely the selection-bias mechanism
  that killed portfolio_v4. If it is pursued, it must be pre-registered and
  tested out-of-sample as its own hypothesis, with the caveat recorded that it
  was discovered post-hoc in this dataset.

--- H2 DXY REGIME: VIABLE TO TEST (the one survivor) ---
corr(gold, DXY) = -0.397 full sample; rolling-63d mean -0.427; below -0.3 for
75% of the time; inverts positive only 4% of the time. Real, strong by
macro-relationship standards, and stable across decades. This clears the gate.
  ** CRITICAL CAVEAT: section 5 measures CONTEMPORANEOUS correlation (same-day
  returns moving together). That is NOT a tradeable edge on its own -- it says
  gold and DXY move opposite each other, not that DXY move predicts the NEXT
  gold move. The plan's own H2 asks "does a DXY breakout LEAD gold?" and this
  study never measured lead/lag. Before H2 becomes a strategy, the next
  measurement must be: regress gold fwd-Nd return on PRIOR DXY move, and check
  the t-stat. If DXY is only coincident, H2 dies too and Phase 1 returns NO-GO
  overall.

--- H3 REAL-YIELD TREND: REJECTED as specified ---
corr(gold, d10Y) = -0.163, weak. Worse, the conditional test runs the wrong
way: gold's fwd-5d return was +21.8bp after yields FELL >15bp, and +30.1bp
after yields ROSE >15bp. Gold did BETTER when yields rose -- the opposite of
the hypothesis. Caveat: this is NOMINAL yield only; the plan specified real
(TIPS) yields, which were never fetched. H3 may be retested with real-yield
data, but the nominal proxy gives it no support.

--- H4 VOLATILITY BREAKOUT: NOT SUPPORTED ---
fwd-10d mean return by vol regime: low +45.7bp / mid +40.5bp / high +45.1bp,
against standard deviations of 308-380bp. The differences are noise. Vol
regime does not predict forward return.

--- THE BAR ANY FUTURE STRATEGY MUST CLEAR ---
Random-entry 95th-pct PF is ~1.27-1.29 at every holding period tested. A D1
strategy must beat that, not merely beat PF 1.0. For scale: the current M15
portfolio_v4's own measured holdout PF (1.18-1.25) would NOT clear this bar.
Buy-and-hold is the other bar: CAGR 11.39%, Sharpe 0.69, MAR 0.26.

--- USEFUL REGARDLESS OF STRATEGY (stop-sizing reality) ---
Median daily true range $5.80; 90th pct $25.70. Note this against the live
M15 system: FVG_NY's ~$5.50 stop is BELOW gold's MEDIAN DAILY range. That is
independent confirmation, from 26 years of data, of why that leg gets
noise-stopped.

--- SEASONALITY: too thin to act on ---
Jan +295bp / Aug +213bp / Jun -104bp look striking but rest on ~26 observations
per month with no multiple-testing correction across 12 months. Not actionable
as-is.

--- NEXT ACTION (single, specific) ---
Measure DXY lead/lag on gold. That one regression decides whether Phase 1 is a
GO or a NO-GO overall, and it is the only remaining live hypothesis.
