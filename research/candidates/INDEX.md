## Notes on construction

- `sl_atr` is sized per SESSION against the measured Phase-1 MAE p50 noise floor, not tuned per rule: ASIA 0.60xATR (~$6 vs $5.77 floor), LATE 0.55xATR (~$5.5 vs $5.16), LONDON 0.70xATR (~$7 vs $6.55), NY 0.90xATR (~$9 vs $8.69, 8.5% of account, marginal), OVERLAP 1.10xATR (~$11 vs $10.14, 10.4% of account, structurally unaffordable and included only so the claim is tested, not assumed).
- Every candidate carries `max_hold_bars` (explicit time stop) and `spread_ceiling` (skip if quoted spread exceeds it) as first-class fields, not defaults borrowed from v1.
- Signals are DEBOUNCED by library convention (not per-rule tuning): session-anchored families (asian_breakout, range_failure, session_reversal, opening_range, noise_band) are collapsed to the first signal per session window; every other family gets a 4-bar cooldown. This is what turns a state-based rule condition into a countable trade frequency.
- H2 (`asian_breakout`) and H3 (`session_reversal`) are logical opposites, run on the same bars with the same cost model, deliberately.
- H5 (self-rescaling noise band = trailing 14-day mean daily range) is used BOTH as a standalone family (`noise_band`) and as an optional threshold mechanism inside `asian_breakout` and `opening_range` candidates (`noise_band_k` parameter).
- H6 (time-of-day-conditioned ATR) is its own family (`tod_volatility`) using `_tod_atr()`, which computes the mean true range of the SAME intraday slot over the trailing 20 occurrences -- a flat 24h ATR is measured to average 10.75 while the time-of-day-normalized version averages 10.86 on the sample checked, confirming the two are not interchangeable.
- H4 (liquidity sweep) always carries a hard ATR stop; the public reference implementation (R1) had none, which Phase 4 research flagged as an unbounded-loss design incompatible with this account.
- Short-only variants (`direction_bias="short"`) are listed separately from long-only wherever the underlying logic is symmetric, because short swap is $0.00/night and long swap is -$0.52/night on this account.

## Verification performed

- `build_library_v2()` imports cleanly and returns 179 candidates with unique IDs.
- Every candidate has a non-empty `hypothesis` and `failure_mode`, `sl_atr > 0`, `tp_atr > 0`, `tp_atr/sl_atr >= 2.0`, and `max_hold_bars > 0`.
- No look-ahead: every candidate's signal on a truncated feature frame matches its signal on the full frame over the overlapping prefix (checked with a 300-bar safety margin against trailing-window helpers), i.e. no rule reads data at or beyond its own bar.
- Sample run on the last 12,000 M15 bars (~125 days) of `research/data/XAUUSDm_M15.npy` via `build_features()`: every candidate produces a signal (no permanently-dead rules after the affordability gate was corrected from a flat-ATR threshold to the H5 trailing-range threshold), frequencies range from ~0.01/day (narrow structural setups such as `mtf_alignment` and rare `tod_volatility` spikes) to ~4.8/day (broad `mean_reversion` and `round_number` families), consistent with the intended mix of a low-frequency structural core plus a wider net of exploratory rules.

## Hypotheses not implemented, or implemented only partially

- **H1** (session-conditional behaviour as foundation) and **H9** (macro-news blackout) are measurement/filtering tasks, not standalone entry rules, and are out of scope for this candidate library by design -- they belong in the backtest harness (news calendar join, per-session diagnostics) rather than as a `Candidate.rule`.
- **H8** (LBMA fix auction clustering at 10:30/15:00 London) is not implemented as its own candidate: the historical directional anomaly is explicitly attributed to conduct that was prosecuted and reformed, and only the volatility-clustering half would be legitimate to trade. It is implicitly covered by the LONDON-session `tod_volatility` and `noise_band` candidates, which do not assume a direction.
- **H12** (day-of-week conditioning) and **H14** (multiple-testing correction) are explicitly methodology, not strategy, per the source document -- H12 is left for a post-hoc screen of `weekday` against realized results rather than as an a-priori entry filter (the source document itself flags calendar effects as the most data-snooped area in finance), and H14 belongs in the scoring harness, not the candidate rules.
- Cross-asset conditioning (R33 -- gold/Treasury momentum alignment) was NOT implemented: it requires a second clean data feed (DXY / real yields) that is not available in `market_study.build_features()`, and Phase 1's data ceiling (M1 begins 2026-05-20) already limits execution-realistic validation to ~3.5 months.
